The practical approach

Good marketing starts with a website that makes sense.

A beautiful site that nobody finds is a problem. Traffic going to a confusing site is also a problem. The work connects the two: make the site useful and credible, make it easier to discover, then use paid traffic when there is a sensible reason to buy it.

01 — Understand the businessWhat you sell, who you sell to, where customers come from and what a useful lead is.

04 — Add acquisitionWhere appropriate, introduce paid campaigns and landing-page improvements with measurement in place.

Questions clients usually have

Search-friendly answers without the jargon.

What is AEO?

Answer Engine Optimization focuses on making useful, well-structured website information easier for search engines and AI-powered answer systems to understand and use. It complements, rather than replaces, good SEO.

Do you only build websites?

No. Websites are the foundation, but the service can also cover SEO, AEO, analytics, Google Ads and practical marketing improvements.

Do you guarantee Google rankings?

No. Search rankings depend on competition, search systems, content quality, authority and other factors outside anyone's control. A credible SEO service should not promise a guaranteed position.

Is the price fixed?

Projects are scoped individually. The indicative levels above give you a sense of scale, while the final price depends on your market, business, existing website and required scope.

What if I only need one thing?

That's completely fine. You can start with a website, SEO audit, technical cleanup, AEO content structure or an advertising review. There is no need to buy a bundle just because several services are available.

Not sure what you actually need?

Send the website or describe the business. I'll help identify the highest-priority problem first.
